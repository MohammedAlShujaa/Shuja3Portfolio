About Me

I'm Mohammed Al-Shujaa, a final-year Information Engineering student at the University of 17 August 1945 (UNTAG) Surabaya, originally from Sana'a, Yemen, and now based in Indonesia. My work sits where data meets software: I build machine learning models, from house-price regression to NLP pipelines with TF-IDF and Word2Vec, and I build the systems around them, like the Laravel and MySQL inventory platform I developed as a software engineering intern at PT Pacific Medan Industri. I hold Stanford's Supervised Machine Learning certificate and work daily with Python, SQL, and modern data tooling.

Beyond code, I teach. I have led English speaking classes for around 40 students at UNTAG's ELCC and taught Arabic to more than 50 learners, which shaped how I explain complex, technical ideas simply. Trilingual in Arabic, English, and Indonesian, and formerly the public finance officer of the Union of Yemeni Students in Indonesia, I bring an engineer's rigor and a communicator's clarity to every project.

Education

B.Eng. Information Engineering, UNTAG Surabaya (2022 to 2026). Supervised Machine Learning Specialization, Stanford University on Coursera.
